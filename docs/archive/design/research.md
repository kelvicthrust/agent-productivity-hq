# Design research

Public references informed the warm visual direction and preserved the existing dashboard structure.
The research examined rendered public pages, source images and guidance. It did not exercise
signed-in analytics dashboards or the running Pixel Agents experience.

## Direction

The research informed a cream canvas, warm neutrals, soft shadows, restrained controls, and small
illustrations drawn for this product. Navigation and filters stay familiar. The charts preserve
coverage and separate scope. Motion highlights incidental transitions and first use, with a
reduced-motion presentation. Public references were sufficient to establish a direction.
