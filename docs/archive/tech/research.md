## Scoped clarification evidence

T6 compared the retained D7 Setup scenes and the product’s historical connected stepper at 1280,
390 and 320 pixels. The accepted translation uses wrapping pills, a numbered current step and a
completed marker. The [Setup fidelity report](../../implementation/evidence/m10/README.md)
preserves the synthetic screenshots.

T6 also examined existing chart redraw measurements. A low p95 could coexist with an
over-budget maximum. The budget therefore applies to each class’s unrounded maximum, and
missing or invalid samples make a class inconclusive. T6 introduced no new native-browser
measurements. See the [chart evidence](../../implementation/evidence/chart-redraw-runs.md).

## Facts still to establish

evidence available in real transcripts, GitHub App minimum permissions, native credential storage,
and the scope and lookback of account-reported usage. The
[historical outcomes](../implementation/execution-record.md#real-source-experiments) describe
which feasibility runs were incomplete or failed.
